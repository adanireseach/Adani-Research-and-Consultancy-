Adani Research & Consultancy ARC

More than a training centre.

Most consultancies stop at logistics — forms, deadlines, applications. ARC starts earlier, with the work itself: sharpening a research question, choosing where AI genuinely strengthens a study, and finding the funding or programme that actually fits the person behind it.

We work as consultants, advisors, and long-term mentors, staying involved from the first draft of an idea to the opportunity it eventually opens.

About the founder.

ARC is founded by Ayan Adani, a dedicated researcher and academic consultant holding a Bachelor's degree in Economics and a Master's degree in Project Planning and Management.

With hands-on expertise in quantitative data analysis (SPSS, Excel) and peer-reviewed scholarly publishing — including research focused on municipal infrastructure and project risk management — the founder brings rigorous academic experience and practical insight to every client's journey.
